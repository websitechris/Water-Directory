# DWI — EIR request (ready to send)

**To:** dwi.enquiries@defra.gov.uk
**Subject:** Environmental Information Regulations 2004 request — Thames Water water supply zone boundaries and postcode/zone lookup

---

Dear DWI FOIA and EIR team,

I am making a request for environmental information under the Environmental
Information Regulations 2004 (EIR).

I am requesting information about the water supply zones used by Thames Water
Utilities Limited — specifically the geographic definition of those zones and how
they relate to postcodes.

The Inspectorate holds this information because water companies are required to
submit their water supply zone boundaries to you. Your own data submission file
specification (Annex A) requires companies to provide, for each water supply zone,
a digital polygon boundary (shapefile / geodatabase), a zone reference and zone
name, the zone centroid national grid reference, and associated postcode,
population and area information.

**I request the following, as held by the Inspectorate for Thames Water Utilities
Limited:**

1. The water supply zone boundary dataset for Thames Water — the digital polygon
   boundaries of its water supply zones, with the zone reference and zone name for
   each zone.

2. Any lookup or dataset the Inspectorate holds that links postcodes and/or Lower
   Layer Super Output Area (LSOA) codes to Thames Water water supply zones.

3. If held, the zone centroid national grid references and the full zone
   reference/name list for Thames Water zones.

**Format:** Please provide this electronically in a reusable, machine-readable
format — shapefile, geodatabase or GeoJSON for the boundaries, and CSV or
spreadsheet for any tabular lookup. Please also confirm any licensing or re-use
terms that apply to the information supplied.

I understand you must respond within 20 working days under Regulation 5(2).

For completeness: equivalent zone information is already in the public domain for
other water companies — for example Southern Water publishes its supply-zone water
quality keyed to LSOA as open data, and Ofwat publishes company water supply areas
under the Open Government Licence — so this category of information is routinely
disclosed. Should you consider any part of this request exempt, I ask that you
apply the public interest test under Regulation 12(1)(b) to the specific
information requested rather than to the request as a whole, and that you provide
any part that is not exempt.

If you do not hold some part of this information, or hold it only in a particular
form, please tell me what you do hold and in what format, and advise me under your
duty to provide advice and assistance (Regulation 9).

If you intend to refuse any part of this request, please state the specific
regulation relied on and the reasoning, so that I can consider an internal review.

Please acknowledge receipt of this request.
