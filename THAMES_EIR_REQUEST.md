# Thames Water — EIR request (ready to send)

**To:** eir.requests@thameswater.co.uk
**Subject:** Environmental Information Regulations 2004 request — water supply zone mapping and quality data

---

Dear Environmental Information team,

I am making a request for environmental information under the Environmental
Information Regulations 2004 (EIR).

I run waterdirectory.co.uk, a free public directory of UK tap water quality. I
already hold the per-zone water quality results that Thames Water publishes for
its supply zones. What I am requesting is the information needed to make that
data usable at a local level, plus the underlying results for recent years.

**I request the following:**

1. A dataset mapping postcodes to Thames Water water supply zones — i.e. a
   lookup showing which supply zone serves each postcode or postcode area across
   the Thames Water region. This should reflect your current supply zones, and
   where possible note any zones that have been merged, renamed or split since
   2023, so that historic results join cleanly to the current zone list.

   If you consider postcode data to be restricted on licensing grounds (for
   example Royal Mail PAF), I would be equally content to receive the mapping
   keyed to Lower Layer Super Output Area (LSOA) codes instead, which are open
   ONS data and to which no licensing restriction applies.

2. The water quality results (determinand, unit, and mean/min/max) for each
   Thames Water supply zone for the calendar years 2023 and 2024, covering at
   least: total hardness (as CaCO₃), nitrate, lead, fluoride and chlorine
   (residual).

**Format:** Please provide this electronically in a reusable, machine-readable
format — CSV, spreadsheet, or GIS shapefile as appropriate to each item. I am
requesting the data itself, not a supervised or in-person inspection of maps, as
a reusable digital dataset is necessary to meet the purpose of the request and is
the form in which you already hold it.

I understand you must respond within 20 working days under Regulation 5(2).

Should you consider any part of this request exempt — for example under
Regulation 12(5)(a) — I would ask that you apply the public interest test
required by Regulation 12(1)(b) to the specific information requested, rather
than the request as a whole. A mapping of which supply zone serves which
postcode, and summary water quality results, is information Thames Water already
publishes at zone level and routinely provides to customers on request; it is not
an engineering schematic or a map of physical assets, outfalls or access points.
Indeed, Thames Water already provides exactly this postcode-to-zone lookup to any
member of the public through the water quality checker on your own website, which
returns the supply zone and its results for a given postcode. Information already
made available to the public in this way cannot reasonably be withheld as a
security risk. There is a strong public interest in people being able to
understand the quality of the water supplied to their own address.

For completeness, I note that a materially similar request for Thames Water
supply-area boundary data was made under EIR in 2016 (WhatDoTheyKnow reference
"Thames Water supply areas - GIS compatible file"). It was acknowledged and
delivery was promised, but no information was provided on the public record. I
raise this only to ask that this request be handled to completion within the
statutory timescale.

If you intend to refuse any part of this request, please state the specific
regulation relied on and the reasoning, so that I can consider an internal review.

Please acknowledge receipt of this request.
